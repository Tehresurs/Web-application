## New structure

- auth_users: unique normalized login, nullable unique employee_id FK employees(id),
  nullable password_hash for blocked uninitialized accounts, blocked flag/reason/date,
  credential_version and timestamps. Active accounts require a hash.
- auth_roles and auth_permissions: unique codes.
- auth_user_roles and auth_role_permissions: composite primary keys and restrictive FKs.
- auth_sessions: unique SHA256-format token_hash, user FK, credential version,
  creation/last-seen/expiry timestamps, revocation timestamp/reason.
- auth_security_events: nullable actor/subject user FKs, time, event type, outcome,
  request UUID and object-shaped JSON details.
